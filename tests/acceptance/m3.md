# M3 manual check: the window shell beside the docs, on a real screen

`cargo xtask accept M3` checks the geometry of every region and control at 1400 × 900 and 900 × 600
(the live headless app and the crate suites), token contrast, the widget-gallery and icon
fingerprints, every menu item of `docs/menus-and-shortcuts.md` §1 in order, and whole-window
screenshots against their baselines. It prints where the three screenshots are
(`target/test-output/acceptance/m3-1400x900.png`, `m3-900x600.png`, `m3-900x600-view-menu.png`):
look at them first. This needs a person and a display, on each OS you can reach.

Keep `docs/ui-layout.md` §2 (the window drawing and the size table), §3, §6 and §11 and
`docs/design-system.md` §11.1 open beside the window.

## 1. At 1400 × 900 (the default size)

1. Start the app on the demo library:

   ```sh
   cargo run -p dalica -- --demo
   ```

   On the first run the window opens at 1400 × 900 points (on a HiDPI screen, that times the scale).
   If an earlier run left another size, delete the demo library's `settings.json` (in `dalica-demo`
   in the OS temp folder) or resize as in step 2.1.
2. Compare, top to bottom, with §2's drawing and table:
   - **Title row, 24 high** (Windows and Linux): the app mark, then File Edit View Folder Picture
     Create Tools Help, the title "Dalica" centred in the free space, and Minimize, Maximize, Close
     flush in the top-right corner (46 wide each; Close turns red on hover). On macOS the row is
     the integrated title strip with the traffic lights, and the menus are in the system menu bar
     (part 4).
   - **Toolbar, 40 high:** Import (wide, with a camera icon), New Album, the Flat | Tree pair and the
     ▾ view-options button at the left; the caption "Filters" above four joined toggles (star,
     face, film, pin) that start just right of the splitter; the age slider; the search field
     running to 64 short of the right edge; nothing drawn at the far right (the activity indicator
     is empty at rest). Controls without a command yet are greyed, not missing.
   - **Middle row:** the tree 240 wide with the "Folders (3)" header and three folder rows; the
     8 wide splitter with its short grip centred; the light table with the Garden, Hills and Harbour
     sections; the 16 wide scroll bar at the right edge. No side pane.
   - **Status line, 20 high**, full width: "No selection", centred and dim.
   - **Bottom area, 96 high**, split at about a third of the width (0.365·W, x ≈ 511): the sunken
     tray box with the "Selection" watermark, its three stacked buttons (pin, circle-slash, album)
     just right of it; right of the split, the star and two rotate buttons, then at the far right
     the loupe, the thumbnail-size slider and the four joined pane toggles (people, map, tag, info);
     under a thin line, the Export ┆ Collage, Slideshow action buttons (icon over label).
3. Click the tag toggle in the bottom bar: the Tags pane opens at the right, 280 wide, with «, the
   centred title and × in its 30 high header, and pushes the light table narrower (it never covers
   it). Click «: the pane widens (to 392 at this size). Click ×: it closes.
4. Drag the splitter: the tree follows, stops at 200 on the left and where the light table keeps 480
   on the right; the filters and the search field move with it.

## 2. At 900 × 600 (the minimum size)

1. Drag the window's bottom-right corner inward until it stops: the minimum is 900 × 600 points.
   (Or, with `--control 7990` added to the command in step 1.1, have your agent send
   `ui.resize {"width": 900, "height": 600}`; `tests/acceptance/m2.md` shows how to connect.)
2. Compare with §11: the "Filters" caption is gone and the four toggles sit centred in the toolbar;
   the search field is shorter but still there; the tree keeps its 240; the three action buttons
   are replaced by one **More…** button (three dots) that opens a menu upward with Export, Collage
   and Slideshow; nothing overlaps or is cut off; the title "Dalica" still fits.
3. Open the longest menu, **View**: every row from Library View to Full Screen is inside the window,
   below the menu bar, nothing runs off the bottom. Hover Thumbnail Caption ▸ and Folder View ▸:
   their submenus open beside it, inside the window. Do the same with Tools ▸ Experimental ▸
   Search for ▸. Escape closes the menus.
4. With the window still at 900 × 600, open a pane (tag toggle): it stays open and the tree narrows
   to 200 to make room. Close it, widen the window to 1400 wide with the Tags pane open, then
   narrow it again: the pane closes by itself on the way down and does not come back when you widen.

## 3. Full screen, window state, title bar

1. **F11 (VIEW-FULLSCREEN, 🟡 until you see this):** press F11. The OS window fills the whole screen
   (no task bar or dock over it) with the same layout for the screen's size: the menus, the
   caption buttons, the toolbar, the tree, the bottom area. Press F11 again: the window comes back
   at its earlier size and place. Then do it through View → Full Screen, and leave with F11.
   Tell the lead what you saw so the parity row can turn ✅ (or say what went wrong).
2. **Window state survives a restart (M3.6):** move the window, resize it to something odd (say
   1200 × 800), drag the splitter to make the tree wider, open the Properties pane (info toggle),
   then close the window with the title bar's ×. Run `cargo run -p dalica -- --demo` again: the
   window comes back at the same place and size, with the same tree width and the Properties pane
   open. Maximize it, close and reopen: it opens maximized.
3. **Drawn title bar (Windows and Linux):** drag the free space of the title row (around the title)
   to move the window; double-click it to maximize, again to restore; the Maximize button shows
   Restore while maximized; the window edges and corners resize it (not while maximized or full
   screen); Minimize minimizes; Close (×) closes the window the way the OS close does, after any
   pending writes.
4. **Native menu bar (macOS):** the system menu bar shows Dalica (About Dalica, Preferences…, Hide
   Dalica, Quit Dalica), then File … Help with the same items as the in-window menus on the other
   OSes (Exit, Options… and About Dalica are not repeated in File, Tools and Help); items without a
   command yet are greyed; choosing Full Screen enters full screen; Cmd+Q quits. Shortcuts run
   through Dalica's own keymap (Cmd+8 stars, Cmd+Shift+Z redoes).

Note anything that differs from the docs on the M3.R check-in, with a screenshot.

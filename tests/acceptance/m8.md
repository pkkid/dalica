# M8 manual checks: save and export your own photos

`cargo xtask accept M8` checks the rest headlessly. The live app (`dalica --headless`, driven over
its control channel) on a fresh library: Save → Undo Save → Save → Revert returning the exact
original bytes, once over `engine.execute` (the shape of the W03 workflow, Save and Revert refused
without `confirm`) and once through the app's widgets (the section header's "2 edited" and Save,
the first-time explanation, the edit panel's Undo Save, Ctrl+S, File ▸ Revert… and the Revert
question); a rotate-only Save turned in the JPEG's own coefficients; Save a Copy and Save As PNG
through the save picker. Two Saves killed while the app is closed (one before its new file is in
place, one before its record is marked done) and the app started again: the folder is repaired,
rolled back and rolled forward, with every original kept and no temporary file left. Then exports:
`export.run` at 320 pixels, Normal, numbered, with and without the location, every file decoded
(size, quality 85 ± 2, camera, date, caption, tags, no GPS when asked); the Export dialog from a
section header's Export with a watermark (in its box, the rest unchanged); a long export's progress
window and its Cancel (only complete files left); the export folders under Projects. Whole-window
fingerprints: `fingerprints/m8-1400x900-header.json` and `m8-900x600-header.json` (the header's
count and Save), `m8-1400x900-export.json` and `m8-900x600-export.json` (the Export dialog, its
location field masked), `m8-1400x900-progress.json` (the progress window, its line and bar
masked). After that: M8's crate suites (among them every format of `formats.md` §1 saved as
specified, a fault at every step of a Save, and the 200-photo mixed export with a Cancel half-way),
`panic_hunt`, the quality estimate's error over JPEGs of known quality, and the `save_export` bench
on small photos in a debug build (times printed). With `DALICA_ACCEPT_RELEASE=1` it also runs the
bench in release and asserts the budgets: Save of a 24 MP JPEG ≤ 1050 ms, its lossless rotation
≤ 815 ms, the 200-photo mixed export ≤ 24 s (medians).

It prints where the screenshots are: look at them first, beside `docs/ui-layout.md` §5.2 and
`docs/creating.md` §2:

- `target/test-output/acceptance/m8-1400x900-header.png` and `m8-900x600-header.png`: the Lisbon
  folder with two edited photos (the edited badge on the first, the portrait third turned), "2
  edited" and the Save button at the header's right;
- `m8-1400x900-export.png` and `m8-900x600-export.png`: the Export dialog over the Porto folder,
  320 pixels, Normal, the watermark on;
- `m8-1400x900-progress.png`: "Exporting 0 of 64…" in the small window under the toolbar's right
  end, its bar and Cancel, with the light table still usable under it.

What only a person can judge is whether saving and exporting feel safe and right on real photos,
and whether other applications read the files Dalica writes. These need a display, a few photos of
your own and another photo application.

## 1. Save, Undo Save and Revert

Work on a copy of a folder of your own photos, on a new library, in a release build:

```sh
cargo run --release -p dalica -- --library /tmp/dalica-m8/library
```

1. Add the copied folder (File ▸ Add Folder…). Open a JPEG, apply Auto Fix and a Tuning change,
   and go back to the library: the folder's header reads "1 edited" with a Save button at its
   right, and the photo's cell carries the edited badge.
2. Click Save. The explanation says the original is kept; choose Save. The badge and "1 edited"
   go. In your file manager, show hidden files and open the folder's `.dalica-originals/`: the
   original is there under its own name, and it opens unchanged in another viewer. The photo
   itself, opened in another viewer, shows your edits.
3. Open the photo again: the edit panel's Undo reads "Undo Save". Click it: the photo shows the
   edits again as unsaved edits, and the file on disk is the original again (another viewer shows
   it unedited; `.dalica-originals/` no longer holds it).
4. Save again (Ctrl+S), then File ▸ Revert…: the question offers Revert, Undo Save and Cancel.
   Choose Revert: the photo is the original, with no edits; compare the file with your untouched
   copy (`cmp` on Linux and macOS, `fc /b` on Windows): byte for byte the same.

## 2. A rotate-only save, Save a Copy and Save As PNG

1. Turn a JPEG a quarter (Ctrl+R) and save it. Open it in another viewer: upright as Dalica
   showed it, with no visible loss (zoom in on fine detail beside the original in
   `.dalica-originals/`).
2. Edit another photo and choose File ▸ Save a Copy: `<name>-001.jpg` appears beside it with the
   edits, as a new photo; the photo you edited keeps its edits, unsaved.
3. File ▸ Save As…: choose a new folder and a name ending in `.png`. The PNG holds the edits,
   opens in another application, and its folder appears in the library.
4. Save a raw photo (or a TIFF) with an edit: a JPEG with the edits appears beside it, and the raw
   file is untouched.

## 3. Export

1. Select a folder's header and click its Export (or File ▸ Export Pictures to Folder…). Choose
   a location outside your photo folders, Resize to 1600 pixels, Normal quality, and add a
   watermark with your name. Export.
2. The progress window counts the photos ("Exporting 12 of 48…"); the library stays usable while
   it runs. When it ends, the toast's Show Folder brings the export folder up, listed under
   Projects in the tree.
3. Open the exported files in another application: 1600 pixels on the long side, the watermark at
   the bottom right, small and legible, the date and camera kept. (Removing the location is an
   option of the Options dialog, which comes later; `export.run`'s `removeLocation` sets it until
   then, and the acceptance test checks that no GPS is left.)
4. Start a larger export and press Cancel half-way: the folder holds only whole photos that open.

Write what felt wrong on the M8.R check-in. Delete `/tmp/dalica-m8` when you are done.

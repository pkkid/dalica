# M11 manual checks: a real card, a real screen, the viewer from the file manager, the quick preview

`cargo xtask accept M11` checks the rest headlessly. The live app (`dalica --headless`, driven over
its control channel) on fresh libraries: a simulated camera card (a temporary mount folder named
by `DALICA_DEVICE_ROOTS`) opened in the Import room with Ctrl+M, its eight pictures listed in three
capture groups, imported into Date taken folders with every copy byte for byte its source and
keeping its time, a file planted under one of their names left alone while the copy takes `-001`;
"Remove the imported files from the card?" asked only once the copying is over and verified, and
refused over the control channel on every route; the card imported again listing every picture as
a duplicate and copying nothing. Picture ▸ Batch Edit ▸ Auto Fix on 100 photos as one undo step,
one Ctrl+Z restoring every recipe in the library and the sidecar; Auto Fix and Auto Contrast on
A│A's right side kept off the photo until "Keep which version?" ▸ Right makes them one step of its
history. The slideshow's keys (→ ← PgDn PgUp Home End, Space, + −, Ctrl+8, Ctrl+R, Esc), the wheel
and the bar (Next, Previous greyed on the first photo, Play, the Transition drop-up, Captions, the
Display time stepper). `dalica --view FILE --headless` opening the viewer on the photo, stepping
with → ← K J, Ctrl+8 starring it into its folder's sidecar, E opening it in the edit view. In the
app on a virtual clock, each of the seven transitions at its midpoint, and 30 changes with every
decode slowed to 1.5 s, none waiting. W10 and W07 through `dalica-cli mcp` over real stdio. After
that: M11's crate suites, the workflow pass rate (`cargo xtask stats --check`, the floor of 11),
`panic_hunt`, and the `import`, `batch_edit` and `slideshow` benches in a debug build (times
printed, the slideshow's zero waits asserted). With `DALICA_ACCEPT_RELEASE=1` it also runs the three
in release and asserts the budgets: 1 000 photos of 2048 px listed and imported (first screenful
≤ 35 ms, listing ≤ 320 ms, ≥ 22.9 files/s and ≥ 2.3 MB/s), Auto Fix on 1 000 photos ≤ 7 ms and its
undo ≤ 5 ms, a 24 MP slideshow's advance ≤ 24 ms and transition frame p95 ≤ 0.3 ms with no wait.

What only a person can judge needs real hardware. Work in a release build, on a new library:

```sh
cargo build --release -p dalica
target/release/dalica --library /tmp/dalica-m11/library
```

## 1. Import from a real card or USB stick

1. Copy a few dozen photos of your own, taken on at least two days, into `DCIM/100TEST` on a spare
   SD card or USB stick (a test stick: step 5 removes files from it). Plug it in.
2. File ▸ Import From… (Ctrl+M). The card is listed under "Import from:" by its name; the pictures
   arrive group by group, grouped by capture time. Exclude two with X; star one and rotate another
   in the preview.
3. Folder name ▸ Date taken, Import All. The progress reads "Copying n of m at … MB/s"; the room
   closes, the library shows the new dated folders selected, and the toast names them. The starred
   and rotated pictures kept their choice.
4. Ctrl+M again on the same card: every picture is dimmed as "Already in your library", and Import
   All copies nothing.
5. Import once more after adding two new photos to the card, with After copying ▸ Delete copied
   files from the card: the question "Remove the imported files from the card? This cannot be
   undone." comes after the copying. Keep Files keeps them; do it again and Remove Imported Files:
   only the two copied files leave the card (the duplicates and excluded ones stay). Edit ▸ Undo
   Import is now greyed with "The originals were removed from the card."
6. Unplug the card while the room is open: it leaves the "Import from:" list.

## 2. A slideshow on a real screen

1. Select a photo in a folder of large photos of your own and press Ctrl+4: the show takes the
   whole screen on black, from that photo. Let it play: each change is smooth, never a grey frame
   or a stall, even on the first pass through the folder.
2. Move the mouse: the bar fades in, and out 2.5 s later. Choose each transition from its drop-up
   in turn and watch one change of each (Pan and Zoom drifts slowly over the whole display time).
3. Captions on: a captioned photo shows its caption on a plate. Display time − and +. Loop in
   Options ▸ Slideshow wraps the show at its end.
4. + zooms and pauses; drag to pan; − back. Ctrl+8 stars, Ctrl+R rotates. Esc: back in the library
   with the last photo shown selected and the window as it was.
5. Play a slideshow of an album and of a search too.

## 3. The viewer from the file manager

On each of Linux, Windows and macOS: open a photo with Dalica from the file manager's Open with
(or `target/release/dalica --view PHOTO`). The viewer fills the screen over a translucent backdrop
(opaque where the platform cannot); Space switches it. Step with ← →, zoom with the wheel, 1 for
100 %, Ctrl+8 stars (the folder joins the library as Scan Once), E opens the photo in Dalica's edit
view in the same window, Esc and close ✕ quit. Once more with Dalica already open on the same
library: the viewer opens read-only, its changes greyed with "Dalica is already open". Note the
desktop environment and OS versions in the check-in.

## 4. The quick preview

In the library, rest the pointer over a thumbnail and hold Ctrl+Alt (Cmd+Option on macOS): the
photo fills the window at once, sharpening in place; turn the wheel to step through its section;
let go: back to the light table with the last photo shown focused and scrolled into view. In the
edit view Ctrl+Alt shows the current photo the same way.

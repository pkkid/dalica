# M7 manual checks: open, zoom, crop, straighten, tune, retouch and undo on your own photos

`cargo xtask accept M7` checks the rest headlessly. The live app (`dalica --headless`, driven over
its control channel) on a fresh library of six `scenes` JPEGs, one of them with an operation no
version of Dalica knows already in its sidecar: every way into the edit view (Enter, Ctrl+3, a
double-click, a filmstrip click) and out (Esc, Ctrl+1, Back to Library); ← / →, PgUp / PgDn, Home /
End through the folder in light-table order, staying put at the ends; the zoom slider's ends and
midpoint (fit, 400 %, 100 %), Fit and 1:1, Ctrl+= landing on 100 %, the refined window while
zoomed. Then every tool on that photo through its widgets: Crop at 4 × 6 applied with Enter,
Straighten +3.5°, Auto Fix, Auto Contrast, Auto Color, a Fill Light drag, a Tuning drag of 24 moves
(one step, one undo takes it all back), Retouch with two patches, Undo Patch and Redo Patch, Ctrl+R
and the flip; after each the unknown operation keeps its text and place, and Undo / Redo name the
step. The app quits and starts again: the history and its labels are the same and redo still
walks; the window matches `fingerprints/m7-1400x900.json` (Basic Fixes) and
`fingerprints/m7-900x600.json` (Tuning); the light table's thumbnail equals the edit view's picture
after downscale. In this process every M7 operation, alone and all together, renders alike at two
sizes, and unknown and legacy operations are skipped and kept. After that: M7's crate suites (every
operation's and Tuning control's fingerprints, two sizes each, the tools, the preview renders),
`panic_hunt`, and the `edit_preview` bench on small photos in a debug build (times printed). With
`DALICA_ACCEPT_RELEASE=1` it also runs the bench on 24 MP JPEGs in release and asserts the budgets
(open ≤ 150 ms, a drag's draft ≤ 16 ms and final ≤ 100 ms, next / previous ≤ 50 ms), and the
Tuning tab's drag in the UI with every p95 ≤ 16 ms.

What only a person can judge is how it feels on real photos: how fast, how smooth, and whether the
fixes look right. These need a display and a few photos of your own.

## 1. Open and move around

Use a copy of a folder of your own photos (edits never touch the files, but work on copies), on a
new library, in a release build:

```sh
cargo run --release -p dalica -- --library /tmp/dalica-m7/library
```

1. Add the folder (File ▸ Add Folder…). Double-click a photo: the edit view opens with the photo
   fitted and sharp almost at once (a large JPEG within a blink, never a blank frame). Esc returns
   to the library with the same scroll and the photo focused. Select another photo and press Enter,
   then Ctrl+1; Ctrl+3 opens it again.
2. → and ← step through the folder; the photo changes at once (its neighbours are ready), the
   filmstrip keeps the photo centred and outlined. Home and End jump to the ends, and → at the end
   stays put. Click a filmstrip thumbnail to open it.
3. Zoom: drag the zoom slider in the bottom bar; its middle is 100 % ("Zoomed to 100 %" at the
   bottom right), its end 400 %. Ctrl+= and Ctrl+− step, and land on 100 % when they cross it. The
   wheel over the photo zooms about the pointer. Press and hold on the fitted photo: it zooms to
   100 % there; a click returns to fit. While zoomed, drag pans (the hand cursor) and stops at the
   photo's edges, with a short glide on release; the navigator shows where you are, and the picture
   sharpens to full detail a moment after you stop. Fit and 1:1 animate.
4. Ctrl+9 hides and shows the edit panel. A double-click on the photo leaves the edit view.

## 2. Crop and straighten

1. Basic Fixes ▸ Crop. Choose 4 × 6 in the size list and draw on the photo: the rectangle keeps the
   ratio, the outside dims, the thirds show while you drag. Drag a corner, an edge and the inside;
   Rotate turns it to portrait. Hold Preview to see the result. Try one of the three suggestions.
   Apply (or Enter): the photo shows cropped, the thumbnail in the filmstrip and back in the
   library follows, and Undo reads "Undo Crop".
2. Crop again: the whole photo shows with your rectangle. Add a custom ratio (Add Custom Ratio…,
   3 × 2 "Print"); it is there after a restart. Esc cancels without a step.
3. Straighten a photo with a tilted horizon: the grid shows, drag the slider until the horizon is
   level (the photo turns live and never shows an empty corner), ← / → nudge 0.1°. Apply.

## 3. Fixes and Tuning

1. Auto Fix, Auto Contrast and Auto Color on a dull, a dark and a colour-cast photo: each is one
   step ("Undo Auto Fix"), and the result looks better, not stranger. Ctrl+Shift+E is Auto Fix.
2. Drag Fill Light in Basic Fixes: the photo follows the slider smoothly while you drag (no
   stutter), and the drag is one step.
3. The Tuning tab: Fill Light, Highlights, Shadows and Color Temperature each follow the drag
   smoothly; hover a slider for its value, + / − nudge, a double-click resets. The Auto lighting
   wand sets the three sliders at once. Arm the eyedropper and click something that should be grey
   or white: the cast goes. All the changes of one visit to the tab are one "Undo Tuning" step.
4. The histogram follows each edit.

## 4. Retouch and undo

1. Basic Fixes ▸ Retouch on a photo with a spot (dust, a blemish): click the spot, move the pointer
   to a clean area (the patch previews under it), click. Make a second patch, then Undo Patch and
   Redo Patch. + and − change the brush. Apply: one "Undo Retouch" step; the spot is gone.
2. Undo several times with the button, Ctrl+Z and Edit ▸ Undo: each names the step it removes, and
   Redo brings them back in order. Rotate (Ctrl+R) is a step of the photo too; the library's own
   Undo is not touched.
3. Quit and start again: open the photo; its edits, Undo and Redo are as you left them. The
   thumbnails in the library show every edit.

Write what felt wrong on the M7.R check-in. Delete `/tmp/dalica-m7` when you are done.

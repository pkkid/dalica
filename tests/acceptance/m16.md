# M16 manual checks: every theme in the real window on each OS, beside its title bar and native dialogs

`cargo xtask accept M16` checks the rest headlessly. The live app (`dalica --headless`, driven over
its control channel) on a folder of eight painted photos: each of the fifteen themes is chosen from
View ▸ Theme ▸ (its row checked after), then by `dalica-cli run --connect` over the control channel,
then by `cmd_theme_<id>` over `dalica-cli mcp --connect`, and every time the very next frame wears
it (the toolbar paints the theme's `chrome`, the light table its `grid_bg`, the tray its `tray_bg`)
and `ui.state` names it; after Pro (Light), the last chosen, a restart opens in Pro (Light); the
Options row previews Studio (Dark) in the next frame without saving it, and Cancel puts Pro (Light)
back. On a folder of seven procedural pictures (a green field, a blue sky, a red barn under a grey
sky, a black-and-white street, a sunset, lemons and lavender): each of the seven colours finds its
picture and only it, typed as `color:<colour>` in the search field, from Tools ▸ Experimental ▸
Search for ▸ and through `query_photos`; a B&W effect moves the field from `color:green` to
`color:bw` and Undo moves it back; the same library with its snapshot rewritten without summaries
opens with every summary made again from the stored sketches, no scan and no thumbnail written.
After that: M16's crate suites (the contrast tests and a gallery fingerprint for every theme, the
main window fingerprinted in every theme at 1400 × 900, the classifier, the index, the store and
the search), `cargo xtask parity` in its review form, `panic_hunt`, and the `colour_summary`,
`colour_search` and `theme_switch` benches in a debug build (times printed). With
`DALICA_ACCEPT_RELEASE=1` it runs them in release with `--check`, each within its budget.

It prints where the screenshots are: look at them first, beside `docs/design-system.md` §2
(Themes) and `docs/organizing.md` §8.1 (all in `target/test-output/acceptance/`, at 1400 × 900):

- `m16-1400x900-dark.png`: the library in Dark, today's look unchanged (eight painted photos in
  Garden);
- `m16-1400x900-proLight.png`: the same library in Pro (Light) after a restart: light chrome and
  light table, dark text, every label readable;
- `m16-1400x900-classic.png`: the same library in Classic: the warm grey chrome, the white folder
  header and tray, the bevelled buttons;
- `m16-1400x900-colour.png` (and `-masked`, the banner's time masked): `color:green`'s results,
  the search album's section with the field above its folder's, the banner "Showing 1 picture in
  1 folder".

The library in each of the fifteen themes is in `target/test-output/ui/` from the `dalica-ui`
suite (`theme-<id>-library-1400x900.png`), and the edit view in Pro (Light) and Classic
(`theme-<id>-edit-1400x900.png`).

Then, on each OS (Linux, Windows, macOS), in the real window (`cargo run -p dalica`):

1. **Every theme, by eye.** Go through View ▸ Theme ▸ one theme at a time (the menu stays open
   between choices only if the OS's menus do). Each switch repaints at once, with no flash of the
   old theme and no restart. In each: the menu bar, the toolbar, the folder tree, the light table,
   the tray and the bottom bar take the theme; text is readable everywhere (the dim captions too);
   the selection, the focus ring and the hover are visible; a tooltip and a context menu wear it.
   Open a photo in the edit view and the Options dialog in at least the light ones (Pro (Light),
   Studio (Light), Adwaita (Light), Breeze Light, Soft Light, Classic).
2. **The title bar beside it.** The title bar is the OS's (or Dalica's own where the OS draws none):
   note whether a light theme under a dark system title bar (and the reverse) looks wrong enough to
   report; Dalica does not follow the system's light or dark mode (M16's *Out*).
3. **Native dialogs beside it.** File ▸ Save As… and Folder ▸ Move… open the OS's own pickers,
   which keep the system's look in every theme: check they still open over the window and that
   going back from one to the themed window looks right.
4. **Classic's bevels.** In Classic, the buttons are bevelled (light top-left, dark bottom-right),
   pressed buttons sink, and nothing else is.
5. **The Options row.** Tools ▸ Options… ▸ General ▸ Interface ▸ Theme: choosing a theme repaints
   the window behind the dialog at once; Cancel puts the saved theme back; OK keeps the new one, and
   it is still there after quitting and starting Dalica again.
6. **Colour search on real photos.** On a folder of your own photos (never committed), Tools ▸
   Experimental ▸ Search for ▸ each colour and `color:` typed in the search field: the matches look
   plausible (a photo shows the colour somewhere prominent), black and white photos answer
   `color:bw`, and a photo made black and white in the edit view moves to `color:bw` once its
   thumbnail redraws.

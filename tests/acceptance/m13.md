`cargo xtask accept M13` checks the rest headlessly, with synthetic face models and no network.

to a loopback proxy that records the host and refuses it) on fresh libraries: a detector planted in
the models folder and a recogniser added from a local file through the face model dialog (Add Model
disabled until "I have read these terms…" is ticked); the face scan filling Unnamed in the

Download disabled and silent until the box is ticked, then a connection to its site. W13 and W14

the public-faces corpus floors when `cargo xtask corpus` has the sets (else said; `cargo xtask

What only a person can judge needs real models, real photos and real hands. Work in a release build,
on a new library holding a few dozen photos of your own with several people in them, some taken
over the years:

```sh
cargo build --release -p dalica
target/release/dalica --library /tmp/dalica-m13/library
```

## 1. The chosen models, downloaded for real

1. Open People for the first time (or Options ▸ People ▸ Download…): the face model dialog lists the
   files Dalica needs with their licence, commercial use, training data, size and site. Download
   stays disabled until the box is ticked; tick it and Download: the progress moves, × stops it,
   Download again resumes from where it stopped.
2. Once installed, Options ▸ People shows the models in use. Add a Model File… with a model file
   you have: its terms (or "Unknown licence" and the stated assumptions) show before Add Model.
3. Remove a model: it asks first, then the file and its face data go.

## 2. Faces on real photos

1. Let the scan run on the library ("Finding faces… n of m" in the People group's header) while you
   scroll, open photos and type: the window never stutters, and the scan slows while you work.
2. Unnamed: the groups hold one person each, the largest first; Expand Groups, Show Ignored Faces.
3. Name a few groups (a new name asks New Person; a known one is that person at once), ignore one
   with ×, drag faces onto a person in the tree. Undo each.
4. A person's section: Zoom to Faces and Show Whole Photos, Set as Person Thumbnail, Play.
5. Rename, Merge Into ▸ and Delete Person from the person menu; Undo each.

## 3. Suggestions

1. With a few faces of each person named, "Name?" appears on faces of the same people only; ✓
   confirms, × rejects (and stays rejected after a restart); Confirm All on a person.
2. Move Suggestion confidence to 95 and 50: fewer, then more, suggestions; Find More on a person
   lists near misses, most alike first.
3. The People pane: hover a face and see its box on the photo in the edit view, and back; Add a
   Person Manually on a turned photo lands where you drew it.

## 4. Names in photo files

1. Turn on Store people names in photos, name a face, and open the photo in another application
   that shows MWG face regions (digiKam, Lightroom, exiftool `-RegionInfo`): the name sits on the
   face. Tools ▸ Experimental ▸ Write People Names to Photos… asks first, then writes the selection.

## 5. The map by hand, tiles, a place search

1. Places pane: drag a few photos from the light table onto the map with a mouse, then with a
   touchpad; the bubble "Put n photos here?" and OK; drag tagged photos again ("Move n photos
   here?"). Zoom with the wheel, the touchpad's pinch and the buttons; pan by dragging.
2. Click a marker: "n photos here", Show These Photos, Remove Location (asks past 5).
3. Allow map tiles when the pane asks: tiles appear over the offline map with the provider's
   attribution; close the pane and watch no more requests (Options ▸ Network ▸ Network log level
   "requests").
4. "Search for a place:": coordinates move the pin without the network; a name searches on Enter;
   the pin's OK puts the selection there.
5. Tools ▸ Geotag ▸ Copy Location, Paste Location, Clear Location….

## 6. A geotagged photo elsewhere

1. With Write captions, tags and places on, geotag a photo and open it in another application's map
   (a file manager's properties, a photo site, exiftool `-gps:all`): it is where you put it.
2. Clear its location and look again: the position is gone, the photo is unchanged otherwise.

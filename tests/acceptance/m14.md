`cargo xtask accept M14` checks the rest headlessly. The live app (`dalica --headless`, driven over
its control channel) on eight painted photos: the bottom bar's Collage with three held photos,
Create ▸ Picture Collage… on the tray, a folder header's Collage and a person section's each open
the Collage room with exactly their photos; the six styles lay the same eight out the same way for
one seed, twice, after a restart and as `fingerprints/m14-layouts.json` records them (within 10⁻⁶,
so on every system); a pile's top picture dragged 80 points left and 30 down follows the pointer
and its wheel handle turns it a quarter, one step of the room's history each and none of the
library's; a grid's first picture dragged onto the second swaps the two and nothing else; Shuffle
keeps the slots and the seed and deals the pictures again; Scramble draws a new seed and drops the
moves, the clips and settings kept; Create Collage renders on a worker with its progress and Cancel
showing, writes a 3000 × 1688 JPEG of the 16:9 page and its `.dalica-collage` recipe in
`Pictures/Dalica/Collages`, opens it in the edit view, and every grid picture's colour in the JPEG
is the one the canvas showed in that place; the folder is the Collages project; Edit Collage
reopens the room with its six clips and settings; a change, then Close asks (Cancel keeps the
room), and Save Draft writes `Collage <date>-001.jpg` with `"draft": true` beside the first, which
is never replaced. On a library of legacy folders written from the documented format: a
legacy-only folder shows its stars, captions, keywords, a recipe `bw()` + `legacy:redeye(1)`, an
album, a person and a geotag after the first scan, which writes nothing; the notice counts the
folders once and closes on OK; the first change writes `.dalica.json` with every photo's data while
every legacy file stays byte-identical, and the folder turns `both`; a folder with both files reads
only `.dalica.json`; the option off takes an unconverted folder's legacy star away (the converted
one keeps its data) and on brings it back, nothing written; Undo Save and Revert bring the legacy
originals back, the saved files in the trash and the originals folder untouched; a legacy sidecar
over the reader's line limit is refused whole, malformed values are skipped and the good ones kept,
without a panic; after a restart the notice is not given again. W15 and W16 through `dalica-cli
mcp` over real stdio. After that: M14's crate suites, the workflow pass rate (`cargo xtask stats
--check`, the floor of 16), `panic_hunt`, and the `collage`, `legacy_scan` and `collage_canvas`

What only a person can judge needs real photos, real hands and a real old library. Work in a
release build, on a new library holding a few dozen photos of your own:

```sh
cargo build --release -p dalica
target/release/dalica --library /tmp/dalica-m14/library
```

## 1. A collage of real photos in each style

1. Hold six photos of different shapes (landscape, portrait, square) and click Collage on the
   bottom bar. The room opens in its own tab with every photo on the page and in Clips (6).
2. Try each style from the style menu: Picture Pile, Mosaic, Frame Mosaic, Grid, Contact Sheet,
   Multiple Exposure. Each looks like its description, the options it lacks are greyed, faces are
   not cut off badly in the cropping styles, and the contact sheet's names are readable.
3. Picture borders none / white / instant on a pile; Grid spacing from none to the most; Draw
   shadows and Show captions (a captioned photo shows its caption).
4. Page format: a few crop ratios, Current Display, portrait; make a custom ratio in the Crop
   panel and see it here, delete it here and see it gone from the Crop panel.
5. Use picture as the background (Set as Background on a picture), then Solid color again.
6. Create Collage: the progress moves, the window stays responsive while it renders; the new photo
   opens in the edit view. Open the JPEG next to the room: every picture is where the canvas
   showed it, sharp at 3000 px.

## 2. The handle and swaps by hand

1. In a pile, drag a picture with a mouse, then with a touchpad: it follows the pointer smoothly,
   other pictures stay. Turn and scale it with its wheel handle: it turns about its centre, the
   size follows the distance. Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y undo and redo each change in the room; Edit ▸ Undo
   in the Library tab never touches the room.
2. Right-click a picture: Bring to Top, Send to Bottom, Delete (back to the Clips; + puts it back).
3. In a Grid and a Mosaic, drag a picture onto another: the two swap and nothing else moves. In a
   Frame Mosaic, Set as Frame Center on a picture.
4. Scramble Collage and Shuffle Pictures a few times each: Scramble gives a new arrangement,
   Shuffle the same places with the pictures dealt again.
5. Get More: the Library tab comes to the front; select a few photos and Back to Collage.

## 3. The eyedropper

1. Background ▸ the swatch ▸ the eyedropper, then click a picture on the page: the background takes
   that colour, and the swatch shows it; one pick, then the eyedropper is off.

## 4. A collage in another application

1. Open the created JPEG in another viewer or editor (a browser, GIMP, a file manager's preview):
   it opens, its colours look the same as in Dalica, and its date is the day it was made.
2. Edit Collage in the edit view's top bar reopens the room with its clips and settings; change it
   and Create Collage again: a new file beside the first, the first unchanged.
3. Change something, then Close: Save Draft, Discard and Cancel each do what they say; a draft is a
   picture with its recipe beside it, and does not open in the edit view.

## 5. A real folder the previous organizer managed

Work on a **copy** of a folder (or a few) that the previous organizer managed, with its hidden
sidecar and originals folder: stars, captions, a few edited and saved photos, an album, named
faces, geotags.

1. Before adding it, record a checksum of every file in it, hidden ones included
   (`find . -type f -exec sha256sum {} + > /tmp/before.txt`).
2. Add the folder. The notice "Found photo data from your previous photo program in n folders. …"
   shows once; OK closes it, and it does not come back after a restart.
3. Check the stars, captions, tags, rotations and crops, the edits (the edit panel's "n edits from
   the previous program (not supported)" where there are some), the albums in the Albums group,
   the people in the People group with their faces, the places on the map, and the folder's
   description and date. Folder ▸ Properties names the previous program's file in use.
4. Change one thing (a star). `.dalica.json` appears in that folder holding everything; Folder ▸
   Properties now says both, Dalica's in use.
5. On a photo the old program saved, Undo Save and then (on another) Revert: the original comes
   back, the saved file is in the trash, the hidden originals folder is untouched.
6. Options ▸ General ▸ "Read photo data left by the previous organizer" off: the folders you have
   not changed lose their old data; on: it comes back.
7. Quit, then compare: `sha256sum -c /tmp/before.txt` — every file the old program wrote is byte
   for byte the same (only `.dalica.json` and the photos you reverted are new or changed).
8. If you still have the old program, open the folder in it: it shows what it showed before.

# M12 manual checks: effects on real photos, the colour picker, the crosshair by hand, Soften, a framed photo elsewhere

`cargo xtask accept M12` checks the rest headlessly. The live app (`dalica --headless`, driven over
its control channel) on fresh libraries: on a 6 MP photo each effect tab's grid listing its tiles
in the catalogue's order with their labels, the frames going on while the previews are on their way
(no frame's UI time near the time the grid takes to fill, the frames that lay out the
opened photo aside) and every tile then holding its preview;
the three tabs and Duo-Tone's open panel matching their fingerprints. On 28 copies of one photo,
each of the 27 effects applied from its tile (Sepia, B&W, Warmify, Invert Colors) or from its panel
(each panel first opened, a value changed and cancelled, recording nothing), each one step of the
photo's history named after it ("Undo Sepia"); every edited thumbnail differing from the unedited
photo's; every recipe still there after a restart and after an index rebuild. On a photo turned
90°, the crosshairs of Soft Focus, Focal B&W, Graduated Tint and Focal Zoom dragged to a point and
applied there, in the stored frame. Soften at full softness painted across a photo and partly
erased, then saved: softened only where the paint stayed, the original kept in
`.dalica-originals/`. A Border at its defaults on a 1200 × 800 photo: shown, exported and saved at
1240 × 840, black outside, white inside, the photo's pixels unchanged within, the original kept, an
export after the Save framed once. Picture ▸ Batch Edit ▸ Sepia on 20 photos as one undo step
"Sepia", one Ctrl+Z taking all 20 back in the library and the sidecar. W11 through `dalica-cli mcp`
over real stdio, `list_edit_ops` listing the 27 effects, all available. After that: M12's crate
suites (a fingerprint and an invariant per effect, at two render sizes and in a zoomed region), the
workflow pass rate (`cargo xtask stats --check`, the floor of 12), `panic_hunt`, and the `effects`,
`effect_tiles` and `slideshow` benches in a debug build (times printed). With
`DALICA_ACCEPT_RELEASE=1` it also runs them in release and asserts M12's budgets: every effect's
24 MP Save within 1.5 × its first measurement and the empty recipe's final on a 2560 × 1600 screen
(p95 ≤ 12 ms) and Save (≤ 55 ms); each tab's tiles of a 24 MP photo to the last tile (tab 3

What only a person can judge needs real photos and real hands. Work in a release build, on a new
library holding a few photos of your own (a portrait, a landscape, a dark scene):

```sh
cargo build --release -p dalica
target/release/dalica --library /tmp/dalica-m12/library
```

## 1. Every effect on real photos, at Fit and at 1:1

1. Open a photo in the edit view (double-click) and go through tabs 3, 4 and 5: the tiles fill in
   one by one on grey, never stalling the window; hover one for its tooltip.
2. Apply each one-click effect (Sepia, B&W, Warmify, Invert Colors) and press Ctrl+Shift+B: each is
   one Undo step named after it. Undo them.
3. Open every parameter effect: move each slider and watch the preview follow (a coarse draft while
   dragging, sharp when you stop); toggle its checkboxes and choices; set Fade; Cancel once and
   check nothing changed; Apply once.
4. Look at each result at Fit and at 1:1 (the bottom area's 1:1): no seams, bands, shifted edges or
   colour casts the effect should not make; Film Grain and the blurs look the same at both zooms.
5. Stack Sepia, Vignette and Border; Undo and Redo through them.

## 2. The colour picker and the eyedropper

1. Tint: click its swatch; the picker opens with the saturation / value field, the hue strip and
   the hex field. Drag in the field and along the strip; type a hex colour; the preview follows.
2. Arm the eyedropper and click a colour in the photo: the swatch takes it. Esc closes the picker,
   a second Esc cancels the panel.
3. The same with Duo-Tone's two colours, Filtered B&W's filter colour and Vignette's colour.

## 3. The crosshair with a mouse and with a touchpad

1. Soft Focus, Focal B&W, Graduated Tint and Focal Zoom: drag the crosshair's centre with a mouse,
   then with a touchpad; the sharp circle (or the gradient) follows under the pointer without lag.
   Drag the extent handle; Graduated Tint's handle turns its angle.
2. Turn the photo 90° and do it again: the effect lands where you put it, also after Apply.

## 4. Soften painted

1. Soften: paint over part of a portrait's skin with the brush; the softening shows only there.
2. Switch to Erase and take part of it back; change the brush size with + and −.
3. Apply, then look at the edges of the painted area at 1:1: a soft transition, no hard rim.

## 5. A framed photo elsewhere

1. Apply Border (or Museum Matte, or Drop Shadow) to a photo and Save it. Find the original in the
   folder's `.dalica-originals/`.
2. Open the saved photo in another application (an image viewer, a browser): it shows the photo with
   its frame at the grown size, its colours as in Dalica.
3. Export the framed photo at its original size and open the export too. Undo Save brings back the
   original file.

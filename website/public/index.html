Features

Organize, find, fix and share

Built for big collections on your own disks, with editing kept to what most photos need.

Organize

Point Dalíca at your photo folders once. It keeps watching them, so new photos simply appear.

  • Watched folders on any internal, external or network disk, chosen in the Folder Manager.
  • Stars, captions and tags from the keyboard, on one photo or a whole selection.
  • Albums that gather photos from any folder without copying them, like the one shown here.
  • File work on disk: move, copy, rename, make folders, and delete to the trash.
  • Import from memory cards and drives, skipping what you already have.

Find

Search answers while you type, across every folder at once.

  • Search as you type over captions, tags, folders and file names.
  • Filters for starred, edited, geotagged, faces and videos, plus a slider for how recent.
  • Search by color: type color:blue and get the sea, the sky and the glacier ice, as here.
  • People: faces are found and grouped on your own computer and never leave it.
  • Places: a map of where photos were taken, and a quick way to place the rest.

Fix

Simple editing for what most photos need, without painting over the original.

  • One-click fixes: Auto Fix, Auto Contrast and Auto Color.
  • Crop and straighten, with common print and screen sizes.
  • Tuning for fill light, highlights, shadows and color temperature, plus a neutral color picker.
  • 27 effects, from black and white and Warmify to Graduated Tint and Focal Zoom.
  • History lists every step, and you can go back to any of them.

Create

Turn a folder or an album into something to sit back and watch, or to print.

  • Collages in six styles: pile, mosaic, frame mosaic, grid, contact sheet and multiple exposure.
  • Slideshows with captions, music and seven transitions, in the window or full screen.
  • Videos play beside your photos; trim a clip or save a frame as a picture.

Share

When photos go somewhere else, Dalíca hands over copies and leaves your library as it was.

  • Export with a size, a quality, numbered file names and an optional watermark.
  • Drag photos out into mail, chat or another app (on Wayland, copy and paste them).
  • Open With any editor on your system, and see the result back in Dalíca.
  • A quick photo viewer for images you open from your file manager.

Your files stay yours

Photos stay in your folders

Dalíca keeps its notes in small, readable files beside your photos, so nothing is locked inside it.

Originals are kept
An edit is a recipe until you Save. Saving a JPEG, or a PNG without transparency, first moves the original into the folder's .dalica-originals, where you can restore it; other files get an edited JPEG beside the untouched original.
Standard metadata
Captions, tags, places and dates also go into the file's own metadata when the format allows, so other apps see them. You can turn this off.
No library to get stuck in
The central index is a cache. Delete it, or uninstall Dalíca, and your folders and their notes are all still there.
No account, no cloud
Dalíca goes online only for things that need it, like map tiles, and asks first. No telemetry, and faces stay on your computer.

FAQ

Before you install

Short answers to what people ask first. The guide explains how everything works inside.

Does Dalíca copy or import my photos?

Not the ones already on your disk. Dalíca reads the folders you point it at and leaves the photos where they are; its index and thumbnails are a cache it can rebuild. Files are copied only when you ask: importing from a card, exporting, or saving an edit.

What happens to a photo when I edit it?

Its pixels stay as they are until you choose Save. Until then the edit is a short recipe in the folder's .dalica.json. Saving a JPEG, or a PNG without transparency, writes the new pixels and first moves the original into .dalica-originals, so it can always be restored. Other files, such as TIFF, WebP, RAW or a PNG with transparency, are left as they are and get an edited JPEG copy beside them.

Which files can it open?

JPEG, PNG, TIFF, WebP, JPEG XL, GIF and BMP, including 16-bit PNG and TIFF, CMYK JPEG and TIFF, and embedded color profiles, plus Photoshop files (their merged picture) and the previews inside RAW files. Animated images show their first frame. Videos play when they are H.264, in MP4, MOV, M4V or MKV. HEIC photos and HEVC videos are not supported in the standard downloads yet.

How large a collection can it handle?

It is tested with 100,000 photos. Thumbnails load in the background, so the window never waits for the disk, and scrolling stays smooth however long the light table gets.

Does it need the internet?

No. Dalíca works fully offline. It connects only when you ask for something that needs a server, such as map tiles or a face model, and it asks first.

I used a classic folder-based organizer. Does my work carry over?

Dalíca follows the same layout and keys. On the first scan it reads the stars, captions and albums such organizers kept in their sidecar files, and it never changes those files.

Is it really free?

Yes. Dalíca is open source under MIT or Apache 2.0. There is no paid version, no account and no advertising.

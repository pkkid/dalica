# dalica-edit

**Layer L1.** The edit recipe: operation syntax parse/print (unknown operations preserved), pipeline order, the CPU renderer for every tool and effect (the oracle), auto-fix analysis.

- **May depend on:** L0 and `image` (`docs/architecture.md` §2; `cargo xtask layers` enforces it). External: `rayon` (the renderer's rows and bands), `thiserror`. Tests also use `dalica-scenes` (photos and fingerprints) and `proptest`.
- **Public API** (M6, M7.1, M7.2 and M7.7; the other operations' renderers arrive with M7.8, M7.13 and M7.14):
  - `syntax`: `Op::parse(text)` never fails and `op.to_string()` prints exactly `text` back, for every string (`data-model.md` §3.1). `Op::Rotate(Turn)` (`rotate(90|180|270)`, clockwise), `Op::FlipH` (`flip_h()`), `Op::FlipV` (`flip_v()`) are typed; the other operations of §3.1 (`LISTED`: crop, straighten, retouch, autofix, autocontrast, autocolor, fill_light, tuning, trim and the effects §3.1 names) are `Op::Listed { name, params }` with the parameter text verbatim, typed on demand by their module (`op.typed()`: `None` for legacy, unknown and module-less operations, `Some(Err)` when the module refuses the text, which is then kept and skipped); `op.label()` ("Crop", "Tuning", "Rotate") and `op.version()`; `Op::from(Typed)` prints typed parameters as an entry; `legacy:…` entries are `Op::Legacy`, and anything else (a newer version's operation, a typed operation with a parameter it does not take such as `rotate(45)` or `rotate(090)`, damaged text) is `Op::Unknown`, both verbatim. A name is `[a-z][a-z0-9_]*`; parameters hold no parenthesis or control character. `op.stage()`: `Geometry` (rotate, flips, crop, straighten), `Adjust` (every other known operation) or `Skipped` (legacy, unknown).
  - `recipe`: `Recipe::parse(strings)` (as stored, no merging; more than `MAX_OPERATIONS` = 10 000 is `Err`, `security.md` §8) and `print()`; `push(op)` appends with the rules of `data-model.md` §3.1: a rotation right after a rotation merges (a net whole turn removes it), a flip right after the same flip cancels, only the last operation ever merges, so an operation between two rotations keeps both; `pipeline()` gives geometry first in the order it stands, then every other known operation in the order applied, legacy and unknown skipped; `orientation()` is what the rotations and flips add up to on the upright picture; `display(exif)` composes it after the EXIF orientation and `display_size(exif, w, h)` gives the displayed size of a stored `w × h` file.
  - `geometry`: `Dihedral`, the eight rotations and mirrorings (a left–right mirror, then clockwise quarter turns): `then`, `inverse`, `from_exif` / `to_exif` (the same eight as EXIF orientations 1–8), `display_size`, `map_pixel`, and `apply(Rgba8)`, which moves every pixel without resampling (the CPU oracle Save uses). A buffer that is not `width × height × 4` bytes is `Err(BadImage)`.
  - `frame` (M7.1, `data-model.md` §3.3): the coordinate frame of stored rectangles and points (the crop, retouch spots, focal-effect centres, face rectangles), which are stored normalised to the photo after its EXIF orientation and before the recipe's rotations and flips. `Point` and `Rect` (0..1, `(0, 0)` top-left, `x` across, `y` down): `transform(d)` maps them by any `Dihedral`, `to_display(orientation)` and `to_stored(orientation)` convert with `Recipe::orientation()`, and a mapped `Rect` always comes out `ordered()`. Non-finite and out-of-range values map without failing; callers clamp or refuse them.
  - `ops` (M7.2): one module per operation family; each owns its typed parameters (`Operation`: `NAME`, `VERSION` (0 while it renders nothing), `LABEL`, `parse`, `Display` for the text between the parentheses, `Default`, `clamped`). Geometry implements `Warp` (`output_size`, `to_input` as an `Affine` of pixel coordinates, `map_point`/`unmap_point` of normalised points); every other operation `Adjust` (`prepare` once per render, with the analysis proxy when `ANALYSES`, returning a `Pass` that runs over `Band`s of `f32` pixels; its `Reach` says how far it reads: the pixel, some pixels, the whole picture). `orient.rs` serves `rotate`, `flip_h`, `flip_v` through `geometry`; `crop.rs` (M7.7: `crop(x0,y0,x1,y1)`, four finite numbers clamped into 0..1, reversed or empty refused, at least one pixel at any size) and `straighten.rs` (M7.7: `straighten(deg)` clamped to ±10, clockwise for positive degrees about the centre, enlarged by `crop_geom::fill_scale` so no empty corner shows; 0° is the identity) render; `tone.rs` (`tuning`, `fill_light`), `auto.rs` (`autofix`, `autocontrast`, `autocolor`) and `retouch.rs` are stubs that render nothing yet. `Typed` is the closed set with modules; `ops::label`/`ops::version` cover every operation of §3.1.
  - `render` (M7.2, `architecture.md` §6.3): `render(Source { pixels, orientation }, &recipe, Target::{Full, Fit { width, height }, Scale(s)})` and `render_region(source, &recipe, Region { scale, x, y, width, height })` (a window of the displayed picture at a scale, equal to that window of the whole render). The source is turned upright by its EXIF orientation; the last `straighten`, then the last `crop`, and the scale are one resample in the stored frame (whole-pixel maps are copied exactly; otherwise bilinear taps over each output pixel's footprint after a box reduction); every other known operation then runs in the order applied, in `f32` bands of `BAND_ROWS` rows through `rayon` (pixel-only passes fused); the recipe's rotations and flips come last, pixel-exactly. Legacy, unknown and module-less operations and refused parameters are skipped. `Geometry` maps normalised points between the stored frame, the output and the displayed picture. Limits: `MAX_PIXELS` (2²⁸) per allocated window, `MAX_EDGE`, `MAX_SCALE`; `Error::TooLarge`, `Error::BadTarget`.
  - `version` (M7.2, `architecture.md` §6.4): `key(&recipe)` → `RenderKey { recipe_hash, renderer_version }` (FNV-1a of the printed recipe; of `PIPELINE_VERSION` and the versions of the operations the recipe uses), so one operation's new version changes only the keys of recipes that use it; `key_with` asks what a bump would change.
  - `crop_geom` (M7.7, ported from LightCraft `crates/geom` @ 76b53778): the maths the Crop and Straighten tools share: `CropBox` (a normalised rectangle in a frame turned by `angle`; `is_within_image`, `constrained`, `with_angle`), `max_inscribed_scale`, `fill_scale`, `crop_fit_angle`, `ratio_rect` (a ratio's largest centred rectangle), `rotate` (portrait ↔ landscape about the centre, kept inside), and `drag` with its `Handle`s (edges, corners, move; the ratio locked unless Manual, a minimum side of `MIN_CROP_FRACTION`, inside the picture). The stored crop uses angle 0.
  - `guides` (M7.7, from LightCraft and PhotoCraft crop overlays): `thirds()` and the straighten `grid(w, h)` as unit-square polylines.
  - `analysis` (M7.2): `PROXY_EDGE`, the longer side of the proxy analysing operations see. `suggest`: empty until M7.12.
- **Test it alone:** `cargo test -p dalica-edit` (no window, GPU, network or personal photos needed). `tests/recipe.rs`: every operation of §3.1 prints back as parsed, unknown and legacy operations stay verbatim and in place, the merge and cancel rules, the pipeline order, the limit, and proptest on random strings and recipes (never panics, prints back unchanged, merging never changes the net orientation or any other operation). `tests/geometry.rs`: every recipe of up to four rotations and flips (781) on a `scenes` photo stored with each EXIF orientation equals turning the upright photo step by step with an independent implementation, pixel for pixel, with the right displayed size; the eight results match `tests/fingerprints/geometry-<transform>.json` (`architecture.md` §11; PNGs in `target/test-output/edit/`, `DALICA_BLESS=1` rewrites them). `tests/render.rs` (helpers in `tests/common/`: scenes as RGBA, the two-size agreement check, fingerprints): every recipe of up to four rotations and flips on every EXIF orientation renders exactly as M6.3's transform; operations Dalica cannot render are skipped and print back; geometry applies first wherever it stands; regions equal the window of the whole render at several scales; the render key follows the versions; labels; point mapping; two sizes agree after downscale and match `tests/fingerprints/render-*.json`; limits are refused before allocating; a 24 MP render is timed; proptest random recipes with hostile parameters and targets never panic and stay within `MAX_PIXELS`. `src/render.rs` unit tests: the adjust stage on a window equals the whole cut (pixel, neighbourhood and whole-picture passes), and the working precision is timed. `tests/crop.rs` and `tests/straighten.rs` (M7.7): parsing, clamping and refusal; two sizes agree; crop and straighten order-free; the stored frame; at least one pixel; no empty corner and 0° the identity; `fingerprints/crop-*.json` and `straighten-*.json` at three values each; LightCraft's crop and drag tests and proptests ported, plus Dalica's stored-crop drag proptest; the thirds and the grid. `tests/frame.rs`: proptest over every transform (displayed-then-stored and back give the point or rectangle back; two transforms compose), whole turns and double flips bring every point back, and on real pixels a rectangle on a `scenes` photo stored with each EXIF orientation and turned by short recipes covers the same pixels in both frames.

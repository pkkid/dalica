# dalica-edit

**Layer L1.** The edit recipe: operation syntax parse/print (unknown operations preserved), pipeline order, the CPU renderer for every tool and effect (the oracle), auto-fix analysis.

- **May depend on:** L0 and `image` (`docs/architecture.md` §2; `cargo xtask layers` enforces it). External: `rayon` (the renderer's rows and bands), `thiserror`. Tests also use `dalica-scenes` (photos and fingerprints) and `proptest`.
- **Public API** (M6 and M7; the 27 effects' syntax and catalogue M12.1, their primitives M12.3; every operation of `data-model.md` §3.1 renders except the effects, whose modules are stubs until M12.6–M12.14, and `trim`, M15, which are kept and skipped):
  - `syntax`: `Op::parse(text)` never fails and `op.to_string()` prints exactly `text` back, for every string (`data-model.md` §3.1). `Op::Rotate(Turn)` (`rotate(90|180|270)`, clockwise), `Op::FlipH` (`flip_h()`), `Op::FlipV` (`flip_v()`) are typed; the other operations of §3.1 (`LISTED`, 36 names: crop, straighten, retouch, autofix, autocontrast, autocolor, fill_light, tuning, trim and the 27 effects in their tabs' grid order) are `Op::Listed { name, params }` with the parameter text verbatim, typed on demand by their module (`op.typed()`: `None` for legacy, unknown and module-less operations and for an operation whose module is still a stub, `VERSION` 0, so Save and the renderer keep treating it as not rendered; `Some(Err)` when the module refuses the text, a stub's too, which is then kept and skipped); `op.label()` ("Crop", "Tuning", "Rotate") and `op.version()`; `Op::from(Typed)` prints typed parameters as an entry; `legacy:…` entries are `Op::Legacy`, and anything else (a newer version's operation, a typed operation with a parameter it does not take such as `rotate(45)` or `rotate(090)`, damaged text) is `Op::Unknown`, both verbatim. A name is `[a-z][a-z0-9_]*`; parameters hold no parenthesis or control character. `op.stage()`: `Geometry` (rotate, flips, crop, straighten), `Adjust` (every other known operation) or `Skipped` (legacy, unknown).
  - `recipe`: `Recipe::parse(strings)` (as stored, no merging; more than `MAX_OPERATIONS` = 10 000 is `Err`, `security.md` §8) and `print()`; `push(op)` appends with the rules of `data-model.md` §3.1: a rotation right after a rotation merges (a net whole turn removes it), a flip right after the same flip cancels, only the last operation ever merges, so an operation between two rotations keeps both; `pipeline()` gives geometry first in the order it stands, then every other known operation in the order applied, legacy and unknown skipped; `orientation()` is what the rotations and flips add up to on the upright picture; `display(exif)` composes it after the EXIF orientation and `display_size(exif, w, h)` gives the displayed size of a stored `w × h` file.
  - `geometry`: `Dihedral`, the eight rotations and mirrorings (a left–right mirror, then clockwise quarter turns): `then`, `inverse`, `from_exif` / `to_exif` (the same eight as EXIF orientations 1–8), `display_size`, `map_pixel`, and `apply(Rgba8)`, which moves every pixel without resampling (the CPU oracle Save uses). A buffer that is not `width × height × 4` bytes is `Err(BadImage)`.
  - `frame` (M7.1, `data-model.md` §3.3): the coordinate frame of stored rectangles and points (the crop, retouch spots, focal-effect centres, face rectangles), which are stored normalised to the photo after its EXIF orientation and before the recipe's rotations and flips. `Point` and `Rect` (0..1, `(0, 0)` top-left, `x` across, `y` down): `transform(d)` maps them by any `Dihedral`, `to_display(orientation)` and `to_stored(orientation)` convert with `Recipe::orientation()`, and a mapped `Rect` always comes out `ordered()`. Non-finite and out-of-range values map without failing; callers clamp or refuse them.
  - `ops` (M7.2): one module per operation family; each owns its typed parameters (`Operation`: `NAME`, `VERSION` (0 while it renders nothing), `LABEL`, `parse`, `Display` for the text between the parentheses, `Default`, `clamped`). Geometry implements `Warp` (`output_size`, `to_input` as an `Affine` of pixel coordinates, `map_point`/`unmap_point` of normalised points); every other operation `Adjust` (`prepare` once per render, with the analysis proxy when `ANALYSES`, returning a `Pass` that runs over `Band`s of `f32` pixels; its `Reach` says how far it reads: the pixel, some pixels, the whole picture). Every module's `VERSION` is 1. `orient.rs` serves `rotate`, `flip_h`, `flip_v` through `geometry`; `crop.rs` (M7.7: `crop(x0,y0,x1,y1)`, four finite numbers clamped into 0..1, reversed or empty refused, at least one pixel at any size; only the last crop counts) and `straighten.rs` (M7.7: `straighten(deg)` clamped to ±`MAX_DEGREES` = 10, clockwise for positive degrees about the centre, enlarged by `crop_geom::fill_scale` so no empty corner shows; 0° is the identity; only the last straighten counts); `auto.rs` (M7.13: `autofix()`, `autocontrast()`, `autocolor()`, any parameter text refused, each analysed on the proxy at its place in the pipeline; Auto Fix is the colour, then the contrast, then a little fill light through Tuning's pass); `tone.rs` (M7.8: `tuning(fill,highlights,shadows,temp,neutral)`, one pixel-only pass, the Neutral and Color Temperature gains then a luma curve; ranges `FILL` 0..1, `HIGHLIGHTS` and `SHADOWS` 0..0.5, `TEMP` −1..1, numbers out of range clamped, `NaN`, a wrong field count or a neutral other than `#rrggbb` or `none` refused; `fill_light(a)` renders exactly as `tuning(a,0,0,0,none)` and is kept as written); `retouch.rs` (M7.14: `retouch(sx,sy,tx,ty,r;…)`, strokes in the stored frame applied in order, each a healed patch from its source with a feathered edge; points clamped into −1..2, the radius, relative to the shorter side, into `MIN_RADIUS` 0.001 .. `MAX_RADIUS` 0.1; more than `MAX_STROKES` = 1000 strokes, a stroke without five finite numbers, or a non-number refused; `retouch()` changes nothing; its `Reach` is the longest chain of strokes reading one another, so a region render grows its window only that far; ported from LightCraft's spot healing). The 27 effects (M12.1, `editing.md` §8.5), one module per family so each renderer task owns one file: `colour.rs` (Sepia, B&W, Warmify, Tint, Saturation, Filtered B&W), `detail.rs` (Sharpen, Film Grain, Glow), `focal.rs` (Soft Focus, Focal B&W, Graduated Tint), `remap.rs` (Invert Colors, Infrared Film, Duo-Tone, Boost), `looks.rs` (HDR Look, Dream Glow, Plastic Lens), `soften.rs` (its strokes `paint|erase,r,x,y,…` after a `;`, at most `MAX_STROKES` = 1000 of at most `MAX_POINTS` = 4096 points), `stylize.rs` (Vignette, Pixelate with `fx::Blend` by name, Focal Zoom), `canvas.rs` (Widescreen Film, Border) and `mats.rs` (Drop Shadow, Museum Matte): typed parameters with their `Default` (the spec's defaults), range constants, `clamped`, `Display`, parsing through `ops::Reader` (numbers clamped, `#rrggbb` colours, `true`/`false`, choices by word, stored-frame points into 0..1; `NaN`, a missing or extra field, a bad colour or word refused) and printing with `ops::num`, `num_point`, `hex`, `flag`; each `VERSION` 0 with a stub `prepare` returning `None` (the size-changing four too, until M12.13 brings their kind). `Typed` is the closed set with modules (`Typed::default_of(name)` gives an operation at its defaults, `clamped()` brings any into range); `ops::label`/`ops::version` cover every operation of §3.1.
  - `render` (M7.2, `architecture.md` §6.3): `render(Source { pixels, orientation }, &recipe, Target::{Full, Fit { width, height }, Scale(s)})` and `render_region(source, &recipe, Region { scale, x, y, width, height })` (a window of the displayed picture at a scale, equal to that window of the whole render). The source is turned upright by its EXIF orientation; the last `straighten`, then the last `crop`, and the scale are one resample in the stored frame (whole-pixel maps are copied exactly; otherwise bilinear taps over each output pixel's footprint after a box reduction); every other known operation then runs in the order applied, in `f32` bands of `BAND_ROWS` rows through `rayon` (pixel-only passes fused); the recipe's rotations and flips come last, pixel-exactly. Legacy, unknown and module-less operations and refused parameters are skipped. `Geometry` maps normalised points between the stored frame, the output and the displayed picture. Limits: `MAX_PIXELS` (2²⁸) per allocated window, `MAX_EDGE`, `MAX_SCALE`; `Error::TooLarge`, `Error::BadTarget`.
  - `catalog` (M10.9, the effects M12.1; `editing.md` §13): `catalog()` lists every operation the parser knows in its order, each `OpInfo` with its label, syntax, `Param`s (`Kind::Number` with its range, `Choice`, `Colour`, `Flag`, `Seconds`, `Strokes` last; the default as written; `log` for a logarithmic slider, Glow's Radius), `changes_size`, milestone, note and, for the 27 effects, an `Effect` (tab 3–5, place in the grid, one-click, `Crosshair::{None, Circle, Gradient}`, the English tooltip, which says when the picture's size changes); `lookup(name)`, `effects(tab)` in grid order (at most `MAX_TILES` = 12), `OpInfo::default_entry`, `renders()` (its module's version is 1 or more) and `typed()`.
  - `fx` (M12.3, `editing.md` §8): what the effects share, on the renderer's bands in its sRGB-encoded working values: `Space` (the stored frame in one render's output: stored points to output pixels and back, lengths relative to the stored photo's shorter side), `Gaussian` blur by three box passes over a `Plane` with its `reach()` (a region with that margin equals the whole blur) and `Blurred` (a pass's `before` view blurred once per render), `Noise` (smooth value noise in stored-frame units, seeded from an entry's parameters with `noise::seed`, mean 0 and spread 1), the masks `Circle` (feathered), `Gradient` (start, angle clockwise from up, feather) and `Edges`, brush `Dabs` along painted and erased strokes and `paint`, the blends (`Blend`: normal, multiply, screen, overlay, soft light), `fade` (`alpha = 1 − Fade/100` toward the input) and `mix`, and `luma` (Rec.601), `to_linear`, `to_srgb`; ported from LightCraft's blur, grain noise and brush masks and PhotoCraft's noise hash (`NOTICE`). Nothing panics or allocates without a bound, whatever the numbers.
  - `version` (M7.2, `architecture.md` §6.4): `key(&recipe)` → `RenderKey { recipe_hash, renderer_version }` (FNV-1a of the printed recipe; of `PIPELINE_VERSION` and the versions of the operations the recipe uses), so one operation's new version changes only the keys of recipes that use it; `key_with` asks what a bump would change; `versions(&recipe)` lists the operations a recipe uses with their versions.
  - `crop_geom` (M7.7, ported from LightCraft `crates/geom` @ 76b53778): the maths the Crop and Straighten tools share: `CropBox` (a normalised rectangle in a frame turned by `angle`; `is_within_image`, `constrained`, `with_angle`), `max_inscribed_scale`, `fill_scale`, `crop_fit_angle`, `ratio_rect` (a ratio's largest centred rectangle), `rotate` (portrait ↔ landscape about the centre, kept inside), and `drag` with its `Handle`s (edges, corners, move; the ratio locked unless Manual, up to `MAX_RATIO` = 20:1 either way, a minimum side of `MIN_CROP_FRACTION` of the shorter side, inside the picture; hostile numbers give the start crop back). The stored crop uses angle 0.
  - `guides` (M7.7, from LightCraft and PhotoCraft crop overlays): `thirds()` and the straighten `grid(w, h)` (square cells of about `GRID_PX` = 32 points on screen, fewer than `GRID_MAX` = 96 lines each way, none for a size that is not finite or under one point) as unit-square polylines.
  - `analysis` (M7.2, M7.13): `PROXY_EDGE`, the longer side of the proxy analysing operations see; `levels` (Auto Contrast: one black point, white point and midtone gamma for all channels), `balance` (Auto Color: grey-world and white-patch gains clamped to 1/1.35…1.35), `fill_amount` (Auto Fix's little fill light); the Tuning wands on the photo's current render at any size, reduced to the proxy size first (`proxy`): `auto_lighting` (fill, highlights, shadows), `auto_neutral` (the colour to pick as neutral) and `color_at` (the mean colour under a normalised point, for the Neutral Color Picker).
  - `suggest` (M7.12, `editing.md` §6.1): `suggest(proxy, width, height, aspect)` gives `COUNT` = 3 suggested crops of a ratio, best first, each a `Suggestion { rect, reason }` normalised to the displayed picture (the caller maps it to the stored frame): the salient subject on a thirds point (`Reason::Subject`), the horizon on a third line (`Horizon`), the largest centred rectangle (`Centred`), then the subject on another thirds point and smaller centred rectangles fill the three places, no two overlapping by more than `MAX_OVERLAP` (0.8 intersection over union). Our own method on an analysis grid of at most `GRID_EDGE` = 96 cells a side; a picture whose saliency peak is under `FLAT` has no subject; deterministic. `analyse(proxy)` gives what it found. An empty proxy or a buffer of the wrong length is `Err(BadImage)`; an empty size or a ratio that is not finite and positive is `Err(BadTarget)`.
- **Test it alone:** `cargo test -p dalica-edit` (no window, GPU, network or personal photos needed). every effect at its defaults prints and parses back equal through a recipe, each number out of range (±1e9, ±infinity) is brought to its bound, and an effect with a `NaN`, an empty or missing field, a bad colour or word, an extra field or a broken Soften stroke is kept as written and skipped. `tests/catalog.rs`: every operation the parser knows is listed once in its order with its module's label and a syntax naming its parameters; the 27 effects stand in their tabs' grids in the spec's order (12, 7 and 8 tiles), the four one-click and four crosshair effects, the size-changing four saying so in their tooltips; each range is what the module clamps to and each choice, colour and checkbox what it reads, the stroke limits; defaults print back and render the same every time; a recipe with every effect renders as the recipe without them (stubs). `tests/fx.rs` (M12.3): the blur keeps a flat plane, spreads a dot by its sigma and equals the whole blur in a region with its margin; labels; point mapping; two sizes agree after downscale and match `tests/fingerprints/render-*.json`; limits are refused before allocating; a 1.5 MP render through the whole pipeline fits its box; proptest random recipes with hostile parameters and targets never panic and stay within `MAX_PIXELS`. `src/render.rs` unit tests: the adjust stage on a window equals the whole cut (pixel, neighbourhood and whole-picture passes), and the fast 8-bit conversions are exact (every `f32` up to 1.01 in full mode;

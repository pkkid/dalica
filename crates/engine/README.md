# dalica-engine

**Layer L4.** `Session`: library + store + jobs + settings + undo stacks; the command registry (`cmd/<area>.rs`); keyboard contexts and the keymap; control-protocol types; demo and synthetic libraries; the session's thumbnails.

- **May depend on:** L0–L3 (`docs/architecture.md` §2; `cargo xtask layers` enforces it).

## Modules: one per concern (M5.5)

`session.rs` keeps the core (opening, `execute`, applying ops, undo, the I/O worker, the per-frame `poll`). Every other concern is a module with its state in a `pub(crate)` field of `Session` and its methods in an `impl Session` block of its own, so the tasks that grow them never share a file:

## Public API

- **M13's ground (M13.3):**
  - Settings (`settings`), each with its typed accessor and setter, wrong types and values out of range reading as defaults and reported by key: `people()` / `set_people()` (`PeopleSettings`, `"people"`: `findFaces` on, `suggestNames` on, `suggestionConfidence` and `groupingConfidence` `CONFIDENCE_DEFAULT` (85) in `CONFIDENCE_RANGE` 50–95 on a step of `CONFIDENCE_STEP` (5), `storeNamesInPhotos` off, `model` the recogniser chosen with Use (`model_id_ok`: 1–`MAX_MODEL_ID_BYTES` (64) lowercase letters, digits, `.`, `_`, `-`; none: the newest installed)); `network()` / `set_network()` (`NetworkSettings`, `"network"`: `allowMapTiles` off, `tileServer`, `tileAttribution` and `geocoder` (text of at most `MAX_TEMPLATE_BYTES` (2 048), the attribution `MAX_ATTRIBUTION_BYTES` (256); defaults `map_tiles::DEFAULT_TILE_SERVER`, `DEFAULT_ATTRIBUTION`, `DEFAULT_GEOCODER`), `proxy` `ProxyMode::System | Manual`, `proxyHost` (at most `MAX_HOST_BYTES`, 253), `proxyPort` `PROXY_PORT_DEFAULT` (8080) in 1–65535, `logLevel` `NetworkLogLevel::Errors | Requests | Details`). The addresses are kept as text; whoever connects checks them.
  - The options catalogue gains `Tab::Network` (`network`) and `Tab::People` (`people`), in the dialog's order between Slideshow and Photo Viewer: the People rows (find faces, suggest names, the two confidences as `Kind::Int {50, 95, 5}`, store names in photos) can be changed; the Network rows can be changed since M13.20 (reserved decisions 4 and 9: `map_tiles::TILES_UNAVAILABLE` and `PROXY_UNAVAILABLE` are `None`; the defaults OpenStreetMap's tiles and Nominatim, Allow map tiles off) and the network log level waits on the network log ("Arrives with the network log"). The face model (status, Download…, Remove, Use) is the face model dialog's, through `faces.models.*`, not a row.
  - Stubs, each a field of `Session` read through `face_scan()`, `faces_index()`, `face_models()`, `face_tiles()`, `suggestions()`, `places()` and `map_tiles()`. `Session::user_activity(UserActivity::Input | Pointer | Idle | Away)`: the window's report of the user's input, noted with the session clock's time (`FaceScan::activity()` → `ActivitySeen {input, pointer, last}`) for the face scan's pace (`faces.md` §4). `Session::set_places_open(bool)`: the window says whether the Places pane is open (`MapTiles::places_open()`); tiles are fetched only while it is. `JobKind::Network` for downloads (a face model, map tiles).
- **Cameras (M15.19, `camera.rs`, `cmd/camera.rs`; `organizing.md` §3; `dalica-scan`'s `devices` and `ptp`):**
  - `camera.list` (query): `{cameras: [{id, name, vendor, model, access: "open"|"held"|"denied"|"failed", reason, storages: [{id, name, capacity, free}]}], usb}` from `dalica-scan`'s cached list (`Session::cameras()` → `Cameras::list`, refreshed off the owner thread at most every 2 s; never waits on a device). A camera another program or driver holds, or this user may not open, is listed with its reason (`camera::unreachable_reason`) and is never taken from its holder. `usb` is `camera::USB_REACHABLE`: this build has the USB transport and the system is Linux or Windows.
  - `camera.open {id, storage?}`: the camera's storage (or every storage) as the Import room's source, as `import.openSource` lists a card; `source.kind` `"camera"`, its path `camera:<id>[:<storage>]` (`camera::source_path`, never a folder on disk). A camera not listed or not open is refused with the reason. The listing runs on the import's worker, never on the owner thread: one PTP session per camera (`camera::Link`, opened on the first device call, shared by the listing, the copies and the removal, dropped and opened again after an error broke it, closed on a thread of its own); thumbnails are the camera's own, else made from the object's bytes when it is at most `READ_CAP` (64 MiB).
  - The import (`import.run`, on the import job's thread; `camera::copy_object`): each object is read in chunks into a new temporary file through `dalica-fs` inside a private staging folder (`STAGING`, `.dalica-camera…`) in the destination folder, `fsync`ed and committed there, given the object's capture date (else its modification date) as its time, read back and checked against the MD5 digest of what the camera sent, and only then renamed to its name by a rename that never replaces a file (a taken name gets `-001`, …). An object the camera now describes differently from its listing is not copied. A camera lost mid-import (`camera::lost`: unplugged, silent, the session broken) stops it with the copies so far kept and the reason (`lost_reason`). Duplicates are found by the same digests as a card's.
  - Removal (`import.removeFromSource`, after its question; `camera::delete_copied`): DeleteObject for one object at a time, only while its copy is still the verified file (same identity) and the camera still reports the size and dates it had when it was listed; otherwise it stays on the camera with the reason.
  - The feature `camera-usb` (on by default) forwards to `dalica-scan`'s `usb` (`nusb`). The root manifest names `dalica-engine` and `dalica-scan` without default features, and every crate that needs the transport asks for it: `dalica` and `dalica-cli` name it, `dalica-ui` and `dalica-mcp` forward it in their own default `camera-usb`. Without it (`cargo build -p dalica-engine --no-default-features`; the cargo-fuzz workspaces above the engine, such as `crates/ui/fuzz`, because `nusb` does not build under `--cfg fuzzing`) `camera.list` answers no camera and `usb: false`, as on a system without USB access, and `camera.open` is refused with "not available in this build"; nothing else changes.
- **Videos (M15.16, `video.rs`, `cmd/video.rs`; `editing.md` §11, `viewing.md` §4):** `video::is_video(lib, id)` (the library's word, else a video's extension; `Session::is_video`); `video.inspect {id}` → `{id, name, path, kind, durationMs, width, height, rotation, displayed, videoCodec, soundCodec, sound, frameRateMilli, playable, reason, trim}` (`video::playable` answers `CANNOT_PLAY` for every video until M15.15 decodes; `video::trim_of` reads the last `trim(start,end)` in milliseconds); `kind` and `durationMs` on every photo of `photo.inspect`, `library.query` and `viewer.list` (`video::add_kind`). The still-only paths: Batch Edit skips a video with `EDITS_REFUSED` (alone: disabled), `photo.history.apply`, `photo.history.tuning`, `photo.tuning.auto`/`pick` and `edit.recipe.set` refuse any new entry but a rotation or a trim (`video::edit_refusal`), Crop and Straighten refuse it (`CROP_REFUSED`), Save refuses it by extension; Export and the slideshow count it; `create.collage` and `collage.clips.add` leave it out and reply `videos` and `notice` (`video::collage_notice`; only videos: `COLLAGE_REFUSED`); the face scan skips a video it cannot play; the thumbnailer never reads one (`Thumb::NotScanned`, no failure: the cell is the video tile); the viewer answers a listed video `Pixels::Failed(CANNOT_PLAY)` without a decode. Tested by `tests/videos.rs`.
- **Poster frames (M15.20, `thumbs.rs`, `preview/jobs.rs`, `faces_scan.rs`):** a video whose codec this build decodes (`thumbs::has_poster`: `dalica_thumbs::video::plays` on the codec its scan read; H.264, HEVC with `dalica-image`'s `hevc` feature) has its poster frame as its thumbnails (the ladder's jobs and cache, a photo's key without edits, made again when the file changes; its scans one priority step below a photo's, no embedded thumbnail; a poster that cannot be made keeps the tile, never the error badge), as its edit-view preview (`jobs::Kind::Poster`: the screen entry, else the poster at the screen size; zoomed, at the clip's own size), and as the picture the face scan reads. - **M15.17, the OS integration's commands (`cmd/os.rs`):** `photo.locate {id}` and `folder.locate {id}` (`Os::reveal`), `photo.openWithList {id}` (`Os::handlers`, Dalica itself left out: `cmd::os::is_dalica`), `photo.openWith {id, handler}`, `photo.openExternal {ids, confirm?}` (`files.externalEditor`, else the system's; more than `ASK_ABOVE` (20) asks first; at most `MAX_EDITOR_IDS` (1 000)), `photo.copyToSystem {ids, picture?}` (`Clip::Files`; with `picture` and one still photo a file job, `JobKind::Analysis` "Copying the picture", rendering its edited picture with `thumbs::ladder::render_edited` at most `MAX_PICTURE` (8 192) px long, `Clip::Picture`; a video goes as its file) and `photo.copyPath {ids}` (`Clip::Text`, one path per line).   - Hooks for M9's later tasks, called by the session: `recover_file_ops_at_open()` and `read_albums_at_open()` from `open`, `albums_applied(op)` and `search_applied(op)` from `apply`, `poll_search()` and `poll_duplicates()` from `poll` (stubs then; built since: the album hooks by M9.10, see **Albums**, search by M9.11, duplicates by M9.16, file-operation recovery by M9.21). `search_state()` gives `SearchState {text, filters, age_days, mode: Search | Duplicates, generation, result}`. `cmd/albums.rs`, `search.rs`, `files.rs`, `rename.rs`, `delete.rs`, `folder_ops.rs` and `duplicates.rs` registered (empty then; built by M9.10–M9.16, see the table above).
- **Albums (M9.10, `cmd/albums.rs`, `cmd/section.rs`, `albums_file.rs`, `scanning.rs`; `organizing.md` §4.4):** `album.create`, `album.info.set`, `album.add` / `album.reorder` (`before` / `after` a member), `album.remove` (asks unless "Remove from albums without asking"), `album.setCover`, `album.delete` (asks), `albums.list`, and `section.sort` on a folder or a user album; each one library undo step stamped with the session clock (Recently Updated). Since M10.29 `Session::execute` stamps every op a command applies (`Library::stamped`) unless it carries its own times; undo, redo and a file job's results are not stamped, and a command's own undo op (Adjust Date) carries the earlier times back (`tests/recently_updated.rs`). Sections `album:<id>`, `album:starred` and `album:recent` are taken by `section.refreshThumbs` and `section.sort` (`cmd::section::section`, `section_photos`); `photo.inspect` lists each photo's albums. Scans put a new folder's sort and a new photo's memberships (creating an album named in the folder, with its `albums.json` definition) into the op that adds them; a rebuild first waits for pending writes, reads `albums.json` on its thread and ends by creating the albums no folder brought back, with their definitions and covers. The watcher's rescans do the same through `rescan_op_with_albums`. A folder renamed on disk gets back everything the model said about it and its photos (`files_op_after`). `edit.undo` / `edit.redo` are disabled with the reason a file step gives. Tested by `tests/albums.rs` and the unit tests of `cmd/albums.rs`, `cmd/section.rs`, `cmd/edit.rs` and `scanning.rs`.
- **Save a Copy and Save As (M8.17, `cmd/save_copy.rs`, `data-model.md` §6):** `photo.saveCopy {ids}` writes each photo with edits as `<stem>-001.jpg` (the first free number) beside it, and `photo.saveTo {id, path, format?}` (the engine's half of Save As) writes a new JPEG or PNG at `path` (format by `format` or the extension; a name without one takes the format's, else the last used; a relative path from the photo's folder; never inside `.dalica-originals`). Both run as `save` file jobs through `create::save::copy` on one worker; the owner thread checks unknown photos, offline folders, unrenderable edits and unfinished Saves without touching the disk. The photo, its file, recipe and originals record are untouched and nothing goes on the undo stack. The copy joins the library as a new, unedited photo and takes the source's caption, tags, star and user date by ops (a copy in a folder outside the library is scanned and reads them from the file). Save As never replaces a file (a taken path fails with `code: "exists"`, a missing folder with `no_folder`, in the job result's `failed`), adds an unwatched folder as Scan Always, and remembers the folder and format on success. Replies `{job, pending: true, total}` (Save As adds `path`, `format`); results `{total, done, written, photos, failed: [{file, reason, code}], cancelled}`. Tested by `tests/save_copy.rs`.
- **Synthetic libraries (M4.7, `synthetic`):** a `Spec` (photos, folders, seed, …) decides a whole library; `library` / `write_index` / `open` build it index-only (100 000 photos with real sketches and no files: written in ≈ 0.2 s, opened in ≈ 0.1 s in release), and `write_photos` / `open_scanned` write the same plan as small `scenes` JPEGs (at most `MAX_SCANNED_PHOTOS`) and scan them. Ids follow the demo library's rule. `demo` builds the `--demo` library.
- **Thumbnails (M4.8, `thumbs`):** `Options { thumbs: ThumbOptions { cache: CacheDir, memory, workers, screen } }`. As scans land, a background job per new or changed photo makes its 128 and 256 entries and its colour sketch from one decode (sketches applied as derived ops: journaled, never undoable, never in a sidecar). `thumbnail(id, need)` answers at once from memory (the covering size, else a smaller one to scale up, else the sketch, else `Thumb::NotScanned`) and queues what is missing; `prefetch_thumbnails(order, visible, velocity, need)` loads ahead in the scroll direction and cancels stale prefetch; `thumbnail_failure`, `thumbnails_pending`, `thumbnail_stats`, `thumbnail_cache_dir`, `set_screen_size`.
- **Jobs and progressive scans (M5.5):**
  - The job registry (`jobs`, `control-protocol.md` §6.13): `JobEntry { job, kind, label, state, progress {done, total}, cancellable, startedMs, finishedMs?, result?, error? }` (serialised as the protocol shows it; times in milliseconds on the session clock since the session opened), `JobKind` (`scan`, `rebuild`, …), `JobState` (`queued | running | done | failed | cancelled`). Finished jobs stay listed for `KEEP` (10 minutes on the session clock) and the registry holds at most `CAP` (64) entries, dropping the oldest finished first (a running job is never dropped). `Session::jobs()`, `job(id)`, `cancel_jobs(Some(id) | None)` (sets the cancel flags and returns the jobs it set; `NotFound` for an unknown or expired job; a finished one is not listed), `wait_job(id, timeout)` (polls until the job finishes or the timeout passes, then gives its entry; it blocks the caller, so the UI answers `jobs.wait` across frames with `job(id)`).
  - Scans (`scanning`) stream through `dalica_scan::scan_streaming` on a thread each and are `scan` jobs (`rebuild` for `library.rebuild` and a damaged index). Each folder is diffed against the model when its turn comes and applied a batch per `poll()`, so folders and thumbnails fill in while the walk goes on; the verification pass (folders gone from roots walked to the end leave the library) runs once everything found is applied. A rebuild builds its model to the side and swaps it in at the end (undo cleared); a cancelled one keeps the old model. Cancel stops the walk before its next file, finishes the folder being applied, drops what was read but not applied, removes nothing, and ends the job as `cancelled` with what it indexed. Files whose header read failed become scan warnings once and are given back to later scans (`scan_failures()`), which skip them until they change. `scan_progress()` gives `ScanProgress { running, foldersQueued, found, indexed }` (`library.inspect` → `scan`); `busy()`; `set_scan_reader(reader)` plants a header reader (tests, benches).
  - `is_new_library()`: no `folders.json` when the library opened and no folder added since (the first-run panel's question).
- **Folder modes (M5.12, `folders`, `data-model.md` §7):**
  - The Folder Manager's rules (`dalica_scan::Rules`: Scan Always, Scan Once, Remove and the faces flag, inherited by subfolders) in `folders.json` v2 (a v1 file is upgraded at open; unknown keys and entries kept). `roots()` lists the units (folders set to Scan Always or Scan Once), `folder_rules()` the rules.
  - Commands `folders.add` (Scan Always and scan), `folders.set {changes, confirm?}` and `folder.remove {path, confirm?}` (one undo step each: folders no longer indexed leave the library with nothing on disk touched, newly indexed regions are scanned; undo restores the rules and the folders with their photos, ids and stars), `folders.browse {path?, refresh?}` (places, volumes and subfolders read on worker threads; `pending` until answered) and `folders.list`. A volume root or the home folder set to Scan Always needs `confirm`. `set_places(places, volumes)` plants the places (tests), `browse_pending()`.
  - `start_verification()`: a region scan of every Scan Always unit and of every Scan Once unit not indexed yet; a Scan Once unit is frozen once its first scan finished.
- **Offline volumes (M5.13, `offline`, `data-model.md` §7, `security.md` §5.9):** online checks of the units on a "dalica-volumes" thread every `CHECK_EVERY` (10 s) of session time and after each scan; folders under an offline root keep their photos, rows and thumbnails; edits are journalled at once and their sidecar writes wait (the writer never creates a missing folder), counted by `pending_writes()` (`library.inspect` → `pendingWrites`) and kept in `pending-writes.json` (`PENDING_FILE`) across restarts, and land when the root returns. `root_online(root)`, `offline_roots()` (for `TreeOptions::offline_roots`), `is_folder_offline(id)`, `refresh_volumes(timeout)` (CLI, tests), `set_online_check(check)` (tests). Mount points (M5.17): a reachable root still counts as offline when it holds no entries while the library has photos under it, or when its Unix device changed and none of up to `SAMPLES` (8) of the library's photo paths under it is there (`dalica_scan::volumes::root_state`; `set_root_probe(probe)` for tests); the offline roots go to the I/O worker (`Msg::Hold`) and the writer writes nothing under them (a flush reports them unsaved). Per-write root check (M6.43): because the online checks are up to 10 s apart, `Msg::Guard` also gives the I/O worker (and the metadata-write thread) one check per root, built from the root probe, the device remembered for the root and the sample paths of the last online check, judged by the same rule as the online checks (`offline::is_library_volume`); it is resent only when the roots, devices or samples change, the owner thread only builds the closures, and the probe runs on the worker right before a write under that root. `tests/offline.rs` covers a root emptied, or replaced by another volume, just before a due write.
- **Watching and external changes (M5.17, `watching`, `data-model.md` §7):**
  - The outermost Scan Always units are watched (`dalica_scan::watch`: the system's events, polling where those fail) on a "dalica-watch" thread; `poll()` ticks it with the session time every `TICK` (100 ms), so debouncing and the poller run on the session clock. `set_watching(WatchMode::Off | Native | Polling)` (default `Native`), `watching()`, `watched_roots()`, `watch_fallbacks()`, `watch_reports()`, `wait_watching(timeout)` (one tick, waited for: CLI, tests).
  - Dirty folders are verified by an incremental rescan per unit (a `scan` job, "Checking changed folders") on a thread of its own once no scan runs, and applied a budget per `poll()` through `diff_rescan`, diffed again if the model changed meanwhile; renamed and moved folders keep their ids, photos and stars. `busy()` and `wait_idle()` include it.
  - The verification pass on the session clock: each unit of `Rules::verification` is rescanned whole `FULL_VERIFICATION` (6 h) after a scan last walked it, `FIRST_VERIFICATION` (60 s) after the first poll if no scan walked it since the session opened, and `RETRY` (60 s) after a verification that could not walk it. With the watcher off it alone finds every change.
  - Every folder a scan or rescan hands out has its stale `.dalica-tmp-*` files removed (`dalica_fs::remove_stale_temps`, at the session time the scan started) on the scan's thread. A walked root holding no entries at all (an unmounted volume's empty mount point) removes nothing, with a warning when the library holds folders there. A photo deleted outside Dalica leaves the library at once; its sidecar entry is pruned 30 days later by the writer (`dalica_store::stale`).

## Test it alone

`cargo test -p dalica-engine` (no window, GPU, network or personal photos needed). The integration
tests are one binary, `tests/all.rs`, whose modules are the files of `tests/` (one file:
`cargo test -p dalica-engine --test all <file>::`; their shared helpers are `tests/common.rs`);
`tests/fileops_faults.rs` is its own binary (it re-runs itself per case). A task's gate runs them
quick (smaller libraries, a sample of fault points, a few fuzz cases); `DALICA_FULL_TESTS=1`, the
review gate, runs them at full strength.

- Every command on a temporary library of `scenes` photos (behaviour, undo/redo restore exactly, disabled cases; `cmd::testing` gives every registered command the hostile parameter sets: an error, no change, no step), and session tests (star 3, undo 1, flush → 2 in the sidecar; a second session is locked out; panics are caught; no file I/O on the owning thread).
- `tests/import_camera.rs` (M15.19): `dalica-scan`'s in-memory responder as the camera through `Session::set_camera_backend`: 40 pictures import with their bytes and the camera's dates, list again as duplicates, and leave the camera only after the question; a camera unplugged after picture 11 keeps 11 and says why. Without the USB transport, `cargo test -p dalica-engine --no-default-features --lib camera::` runs `camera::tests::a_build_without_the_usb_transport_lists_no_camera` (`camera.list` → no camera, `usb: false`; `camera.open` refused with the reason).
- `tests/keymap.rs`; `tests/synthetic.rs` (same seed, same library; the 100 k preset; `--release -- --nocapture` prints its timings); `tests/thumbs.rs` (one decode per photo, answers that never wait, prefetch, no disk I/O on the owner thread).
- `tests/progressive_scan.rs`: 2 000 photos in 40 folders (800 in 4 in a task's gate), the walk held at the last folder by a gated header reader: the first batch lands with its thumbnail jobs queued before the walk ends, no poll applies more than one batch, the progress counts rise to the totals, and the owner thread makes no `fs` call; a scan cancelled while reading the middle folder reads nothing past it and keeps the folders it applied; a cancelled rebuild keeps the old model; a failing file is reported once and not read again until it changes.
- `tests/jobs.rs`: a scan listed with its progress and result in the protocol's shape; expiry after 10 minutes of virtual time; the cap of 64; `cancel(all)` and a `wait` that times out; `NotFound` for unknown jobs; `is_new_library`.
- `tests/folders.rs`: modes and inheritance, `folders.json` v1 → v2, undo of a mode change, Scan Once frozen, browsing on workers. `tests/offline.rs`: a volume unplugged by renaming its root stays with its photos and thumbnails, an edit meanwhile waits and lands on return (also across restarts), a hung or panicking check never blocks `poll`.
- `tests/watching.rs`: on temp libraries while the session runs, files and folders added, removed, renamed, rewritten and moved reach the library through the polling watcher on virtual time (one smoke test on the system's events, real time); with the watcher off the verification pass alone finds them; a moved folder keeps its stars; changes made while closed are found after the first poll; a stale temporary file is removed and a fresh one kept; a missing photo's entry survives 29 days and is gone after 31; an emptied root removes nothing; the owner thread makes no `fs` call.

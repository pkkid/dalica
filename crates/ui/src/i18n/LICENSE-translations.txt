They are original translations, written from the meaning of Dalica's own English text. No
localisation file, string table or translated screenshot of the reference organizer, or of any
other proprietary product, was consulted or copied (docs/localization.md, Clean-room
translations).

Thai (th.tsv): the Dalica contributors, 2026. A machine-assisted draft, written with an AI
model (Claude, Anthropic) from Dalica's English text, every entry marked `~` until a fluent
speaker has reviewed it (docs/localization-th.md).
